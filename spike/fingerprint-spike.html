How to read a print

Pattern typeFighting style. Striker = whorl, Wrestler = loop, Grappler = double loop, Brawler = tented arch, Balanced = plain arch.
Ridge spacingPace. More significant strikes landed per minute means tighter, busier ridges.
Ink pressureFinish rate. Finishers press hard and leave bold, heavy ridges. Decision fighters leave a light touch.
Worn, broken ridgesDamage absorbed per minute. Fighters who eat a lot of shots have worn-down prints, like a labourer's hands.
CreasesEvery KO/TKO loss leaves a permanent crease cutting across the ridges.
Minutiae (ridge splits & endings)Every fight in their career. Read it like tree rings: debut at the core, latest fight at the edge. Turn on Forensic view to see them marked.
Left or right handStance. Orthodox leaves a right-hand print, southpaw a mirrored left-hand print, switch stance stands upright.
Core heightWhere they aim. Head hunters' cores sit high, body and leg kickers' cores sit low.
Print sizeReach.
Fine detailsSeeded from the fighter's ID, so no two prints are ever identical.
